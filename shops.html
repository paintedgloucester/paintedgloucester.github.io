Shops & studios

Buy local, learn local

Independent shops and nonprofits keep Cape Ann's art community going. Each listing shows the month it was last checked against its sources. Hours change with the seasons, so call ahead. See them on the street map.

Art supplies

Island Art & Hobby

117 Main Street, Gloucester, MA 01930 · (978) 879-4511

Downtown Gloucester's combined art, craft and hobby shop: artist paints, brushes and surfaces alongside model kits and craft supplies.

  • The closest full art-supply counter to the harbor painting sites, a short walk from the Cape Ann Museum.
  • A local review reports the owner will special-order items the shop doesn't stock.
  • Children's art classes have been held at the store in past years.

Listed hours are from an older fall schedule (Mon–Sat 10–6, Sun 12–5). Call before you go.

Rusty and Ingrid Art & Supply

18 Bearskin Neck, Rockport, MA 01966

An artist-run supply shop on Bearskin Neck, stocked for beginners and working painters, from the printmakers behind Rusty and Ingrid Creative Company.

  • A free Saturday-morning sketch walk led by artist Rusty Kinnunen has started from the shop.
  • The owners' limited-edition screen prints of Cape Ann are sold a few doors away on the Neck.
  • Two minutes' walk from Motif No. 1 and the T-Wharf views.

Sources disagree on which Bearskin Neck number is the supply shop and which is the print shop, and hours are seasonal. Check before visiting.

Art Supplies Wholesale

4 Enon Street, North Beverly, MA 01915 · (978) 922-2420

A long-running North Shore supplier of professional artist materials, also serving schools and institutions.

  • Handles bulk orders and school or art-course supply lists.
  • The best-stocked stop on Route 1A for painters driving up to Cape Ann from Boston.

One directory gives a Marblehead address. Directory hours: Mon–Fri 9–5, Sat 10–5, closed Sunday. Confirm before you go.

Blick Art Materials, Central Square

619 Massachusetts Avenue, Cambridge, MA · further afield

The nearest large chain art store, about an hour south of Gloucester. Worth a stop for specialist pigments, large canvases and pochade boxes before you drive up.

  • Large stock of plein-air easels, panels and professional oil and watercolor lines.

Chain store; check the store page for current hours.

Workshops & associations

Rockport Art Association & Museum

12 Main Street, Rockport, MA 01966

A century-old artists' association and museum, with exhibitions and the Cape Ann Summer School of Art.

  • Multi-day plein-air workshops: the 2026 program included Harley Bartlett's “The Painted Sketch of Rockport” (Sept 8–12) and Karen Blackwood's sea-painting workshop (Sept 21–25).
  • Adult classes, including plein-air watercolor workshops.

North Shore Arts Association

11 Pirates Lane, East Gloucester, MA 01930

A historic artists' association in a waterfront barn on East Gloucester's harbor, founded during the art-colony years.

  • Member exhibitions, demonstrations and classes, steps from the Rocky Neck painting grounds.

Rocky Neck Art Colony

Cultural Center, 6 Wonson Street, Gloucester, MA 01930 · 978-515-7004

One of America's oldest working art colonies: a neighborhood of studios and galleries on the East Gloucester peninsula that drew Twachtman, Hopper and Stuart Davis.

  • Workshops and classes for all skill levels, taught by working Rocky Neck artists at the Cultural Center. Check the calendar for what is running.
  • Co-hosts a Paint Out on Rocky Neck with Cape Ann Plein Air and Cape Ann Art Haven.
  • The public way on Rocky Neck looks west across the harbor at Homer's sunsets and Ten Pound Island.

Cape Ann Art Haven

180B Main Street, Gloucester, MA 01930 · 978-283-3888

A nonprofit community art center downtown, with painting, drawing, pottery and printmaking classes for kids, teens and families.

  • Family open studios and all-ages classes, plus portfolio prep for students applying to art school.
  • Co-host of the Paint Out on Rocky Neck.

Some listings give 20 Maplewood Ave. Check arthaven.org for the current address and schedule.

Museums & galleries

Cape Ann Museum

27 Pleasant Street, Gloucester, MA 01930

Home of the largest public collection of Fitz Henry Lane's paintings and drawings, including Ten Pound Island and The Western Shore with Norman's Woe.

  • See the Lane canvases in person before painting the same harbor views.
  • Lane's own studio stood a few blocks away, above the harbor on Duncan's Point.

Paint-outs and annual events

Avery, Gottlieb & Rothko: By the Sea

October 24, 2026 – January 24, 2027 (shown at the Cape Ann Museum June 30 – September 27, 2026). · The Phillips Collection, Washington, DC

The Cape Ann Museum's exhibition on the summers Milton Avery, Adolph Gottlieb and Mark Rothko spent painting together in Gloucester in the 1930s and 1940s, now on tour. The catalogue (Rizzoli, 2026) is edited by Eliza Rathbone.

Checked November 2026 · The Phillips Collection 2026–27 schedule ↗

Cape Ann Plein Air

Early October, annually. 2026: October 3–11 (10th anniversary). · Gloucester, Rockport, Essex and Manchester-by-the-Sea

A week-long festival in which invited master painters work outdoors across Cape Ann, with public paint-outs, demonstrations and a sale.

Motif No. 1 Day paint-out

Mid-May, annually. 2026: Saturday, May 16, 11 a.m.–3 p.m. · Barletta Park, Rockport

A town celebration of the red fishing shack known as the most-painted building in America, with an open paint-out.
