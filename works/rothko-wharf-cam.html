Source · note 151

Mark Rothko, Untitled (wharf, Gloucester, Massachusetts), 1934, watercolor on construction paper, 12 1/4 × 14 15/16 in., Cape Ann Museum, Gloucester, Gift of Christopher Rothko, 2026.022.001, accessed November 2026, https://rothko.nga.gov/Detail/objects/2486. Data-verified from Mark Rothko: Works on Paper, National Gallery of Art (estate no. 1076.25–27; provenance Collection of Christopher Rothko, 1988–2026). The catalogue identifies the subject as the Reed and Gamage wharfs on the Inner Harbor near Smith Cove, painted from Banner Hill, and notes that Gottlieb sketched the same wharf from a similar vantage. The gift, the museum’s first Rothko, was reported by WBUR, “Cape Ann Museum gifted Rothko painting that depicts Gloucester,” August 24, 2026, https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/08/24/cape-ann-museum-rothko-donation-gloucester, which describes the medium as watercolor and gouache.
