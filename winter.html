Painting in winter

Most painters leave Cape Ann in October. They miss the year’s lowest, warmest light, the empty beaches and, on the coldest mornings, sea smoke over the harbor.

  • 24°noon sun at the winter solstice, against 71° in June
  • 4:08p.m., the earliest sunset, around December 8
  • 9 h 01 mof daylight on December 21, against 15 h 18 m in June
  • WSWwhere the solstice sun sets (238°), far round to the left of the summer sunset

Why the light is different

At midday in December the sun is about as high as it is at 5:55 p.m. in mid-July, so every hour of a winter day has the long shadows and raking light that summer painters wait for in the evening.

Sea smoke

Sea smoke forms when very cold air moves over water that is much warmer than it, usually when the air is below about 10°F. The water evaporates and condenses at once into fog that rises in columns1. Look for it on the first still, clear morning after an Arctic cold front, just after sunrise, from Stacy Boulevard or the Rocky Neck causeway.

Winter painters

Lane painted the harbor frozen in the 1850s. Aldro Hibbard, who taught in Rockport, painted it under snow; the Museum of Fine Arts bought his Winter Days in 19202, and the Smithsonian American Art Museum owns his Rockport Winter3.

What’s open in winter

Many Cape Ann galleries close or shorten their hours from November to April. Here is what the guide’s listings say for the winter months. Hours change, so call before a long drive.

  • Island Art & Hobby · Gloucester
    Status uncertain. Call before you go.
  • Rusty and Ingrid Art & Supply · Rockport
    Uncertain. The Dock Square gallery lists daily hours of 11–5.
  • Art Supplies Wholesale · Beverly
    Directory hours are year-round.
  • Blick Art Materials, Central Square · Cambridge
    Open year-round.
  • Cape Ann Museum · Gloucester
    The Downtown Campus is open year-round. The CAM Green campus on Poplar Street is seasonal.
  • Rockport Art Association & Museum · Rockport
    Closed to the public January–March.
  • North Shore Arts Association · Gloucester
    Closed for the season; reopens in May 2027.
  • Rocky Neck Art Colony · Gloucester
    The Cultural Center keeps its weekend hours, but many Rocky Neck galleries open only from May to October.
  • Local Colors Artists' Cooperative · Gloucester
    Open year-round; the gallery lists these as temporary hours.
  • Montserrat College of Art · Beverly
    Open during the academic year; check for breaks between exhibitions.

Working in the cold

  • Oil paint keeps working in the cold; watercolor and acrylic freeze. If you use water media, work small and fast and keep the water in a vacuum flask.
  • Paint from the car on the worst days. Several historic views are close to public parking, among them Stacy Boulevard and Rocky Neck.
  • Fingerless gloves under mittens, two pairs of socks and a foam pad to stand on.
  • Ice makes the granite ledges and the Dog Bar Breakwater dangerous. Stay off them when they are wet or frozen.
  • The sun sets as early as 4:08 p.m. in early December (earliest around December 8). Plan to finish by about 3:30 p.m. and pack up in daylight.
