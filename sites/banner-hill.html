Where they stood

Banner Hill is the ridge above Smith Cove, from which “many of the most notable American impressionist landscapes of Gloucester were painted.”141 Metcalf's Gloucester Harbor was “painted from Banner Hill looking westward”; Hassam's 1899 view was taken from the same hill, “a little farther to the left” than Duveneck's.141138 The marker is the hill's road (OpenStreetMap); the exact spots are not recorded.

The light

Metcalf painted “with the morning sun at the artist's back,” so the town across the harbor is lit full-face.141 At 9 a.m. in early August the sun stands in the east, about 36° up, behind and to the right of a painter facing downtown.

Paint it today

  • The Banner Hill vantage is now private property and not open to the public; the view can be seen from the road.138
  • Go in the morning. In the afternoon the town is backlit and the harbor turns to glare.
  • Rocky Neck's Art Trail marks this and other historic painting sites.136
