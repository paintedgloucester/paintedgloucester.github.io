Where they stood

In summer 1880 Homer boarded on Ten Pound Island with the lighthouse keeper, Oliver Merrill.169175 Princeton places Eastern Point Light in the series “painted by Homer on Ten Pound Island.”170 Where on the island he sat is not recorded; the line on the map runs from the island's light to Eastern Point Light, almost due south.

The light

Eastern Point Light is a moonlit night: rapid blue washes for the sky, the full moon “scraped out,” the water dappled by gouging with the brush end.170 Most of the series are sunsets over the western shore;174 in early August the sun sets in the west-northwest, to the painter's right when facing the harbor mouth.

Paint it today

  • The island is owned by the City of Gloucester, which keeps walking paths to the light; private boaters may land, but there is no landing facility. The tower itself is Coast Guard property and closed.175
  • Without a boat, paint the island and Homer's sunsets from the Rocky Neck public way, looking west across the harbor.174
  • Homer's 1880 method, per the MFA: let paint soak into the paper, blot it out, then lay on thick opaque dashes.169
