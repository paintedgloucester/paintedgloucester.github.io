Where they stood

Twachtman painted Harbor View Hotel “from the end of the long pier,” the hotel's float on Wonson's Cove, looking back at the hotel with his cottage studio at far left. It was on his easel when he died there in August 1902.64 The hotel stood at what is now 21 Eastern Point Road.180 The pier's end is placed at the middle of the cove.

The light

Not described in the sources. Facing the hotel from the cove is roughly an eastward view, so late-afternoon sun would fall full on the buildings from behind the painter.

Paint it today

  • The hotel is gone; the site is Rocky Neck Art Trail stop 15, reached on foot from Rocky Neck Avenue.180
  • The painting is at the Nelson-Atkins Museum (33-57). Its image is not reproduced here, so look at it on the museum's page before you go.66
