The nine painting sites

Everything the chart shows, as a list: where each painter stood, what they were looking at, which way to face and a directions link. Open the chart for the sun planner and the camera overlay.

Check the tide table (NOAA station 8441841; mean range about 8.8 ft) and paint from public ways: several historic vantage points are now private land or reachable only by water. Each site's page has the access notes.

Printable chart of the nine sites (PDF, one page)

  1. 1

    Fort Point, looking at Ten Pound Island

    Fitz Henry Lane, Ten Pound Island, Gloucester, 1850s · Vantage documented

    Stand at Fort Point (42.60840, -70.66327). Face SSW, 194°, toward Ten Pound Island Light, 750 m (2,460 ft) away.

    Directions ↗Access notes and lightOn the chart

    Ten Pound Island, looking out the harbor

    Winslow Homer, Eastern Point Light, 1880 · Vantage documented

    Stand at Ten Pound Island (42.60187, -70.66555). Face S, 178°, toward Eastern Point Light, 2.4 km (1.5 mi) away.

    Directions ↗Access notes and lightOn the chart

  3. 3

    Banner Hill, above Smith Cove

    Willard Metcalf and Childe Hassam, Gloucester Harbor, 1895 · Area documented · spot approximate

    Stand at Banner Hill (42.60644, -70.65084). Face NW, 311°, toward Downtown (City Hall), 1.3 km (0.8 mi) away.

    Directions ↗Access notes and lightOn the chart

    Brace's Cove, Eastern Point

    Fitz Henry Lane, Brace's Rock, Eastern Point, Gloucester, c. 1864 · Area documented · spot approximate

    Stand at north shore of the cove (42.59289, -70.64994). Face SSE, 166°, toward Brace's Rock, 668 m (2,190 ft) away.

    Directions ↗Access notes and lightOn the chart

    Norman's Woe, the western shore

    Fitz Henry Lane, The Western Shore with Norman's Woe, 1862 · Area documented · spot approximate

    Stand at shore north of the rock (42.58434, -70.69275). Face S, 183°, toward Norman's Woe Rock, 500 m (1,641 ft) away.

How the vantage points are tagged

TagWhat it means
Vantage documentedA catalogue raisonné, inscription or published study fixes where the painter stood.
Area documented · spot approximateThe sources name the place; the exact spot is placed at the nearest mapped shore point.
Secondary source · spot approximateOnly a later secondary account places the painter.
Modern viewpoint · our suggestionNo source says where the painter stood; the marker is a public viewpoint we suggest.
