Where they stood

The title places the picture at Lighthouse Beach beside Annisquam Light, on Wigwam Point.90 Jones's exact spot is not recorded, and the museum page could not be checked for it. The line on the map is a modern alternative: the light can be seen from Wingaersheek Beach across the river mouth.183

The light

No source describes the time of day. From Wingaersheek you look north at the light: morning sun is side-lit from the right, and afternoon sun comes over your left shoulder onto the tower.

Paint it today

  • The light's keeper's house is Coast Guard housing; the light can be viewed from Wingaersheek Beach.183
  • Non-resident beach parking must be reserved in summer (Memorial Day to Labor Day).163
  • Jones's sand and ledge change with the tide. At low water the flats here widen a great deal.
