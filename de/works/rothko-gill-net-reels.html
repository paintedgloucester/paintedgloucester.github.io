Mark Rothko, [Figures with gill-net reels], 1934, watercolor, graphite and scraping on watercolor paper, 22 3/8 × 15 5/16 in., National Gallery of Art, Washington, Gift of The Mark Rothko Foundation, Inc., 1986.43.206.b, accessed November 2026, https://rothko.nga.gov/Detail/objects/314. Data-verified from Mark Rothko: Works on Paper, which places it at the wharfs of Smith Cove, the center of the city’s gill-net fishing, and compares Arthur Rothstein’s 1937 photograph Dock at Gloucester, Massachusetts (Library of Congress). The other side of the sheet is [Girl on red sofa], 1934/1935.
