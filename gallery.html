Painted at the sites

Paintings made at the guide’s sites by visitors and local painters. Every entry is sent in by the painter, checked by hand and published with their permission.

Send in a painting ↗

How to send a painting

  1. Paint at one of the nine sites, on a trail, or anywhere on Cape Ann.
  2. Photograph it square-on in daylight, without glare or frame, and crop to the edges of the painting.
  3. Open the submission form and drag the photo into it. The form asks for the title, the date, the medium and where you painted it, and for your permission to publish.
  4. We check every entry by hand and add it here, usually within a few weeks. We will reply on the form if anything is missing.
Before you sendThe form is on GitHub, so you need a free GitHub account, and your GitHub username and the form are public. Use the name you want credited, not your address, phone number or email. Do not send paintings that show people who have not agreed to be shown, and if you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to send it for you.

By sending a painting you confirm that you made it and that you allow Painted Gloucester to show the photograph on this site, credited to you. You keep the copyright. To have a painting taken down, reply on its form or send a correction through the corrections form.
