Fuente · nota 152

Mark Rothko, [Figures playing handball on the beach], 1934, watercolor on watercolor paper, 15 1/4 × 11 3/8 in., National Gallery of Art, Washington, Gift of The Mark Rothko Foundation, Inc., 1986.56.538.a, accessed November 2026, https://rothko.nga.gov/Detail/objects/283. Data-verified from Mark Rothko: Works on Paper. The catalogue dates it to the summer of 1934 in Gloucester and quotes Edith (Sachar) Carson, interviewed by Walter Hopps in 1976, recalling that the friends met on the beach daily to swim and play handball. The reverse is [Schooner at wharf] (note 153).
