Mary Blood Mellen, Gloucester Harbor (Laying Nets), about 1870, oil on canvas, 24 3/16 × 36 1/8 in., Bowdoin College Museum of Art, Brunswick, Maine, 1971.42, accessed November 2026, https://artmuseum.bowdoin.edu/Obj7699. Page-verified. Gift of Mrs. Hope P. Gilmor. The museum lists the work as public domain and records its earlier attribution to Fitz Henry Lane; Mellen was Lane's student and studio collaborator in Gloucester in the 1850s.
