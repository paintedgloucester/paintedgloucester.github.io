Fonte · nota 146

Mary Blood Mellen, Field Beach, Stage Fort Park, c. 1850, oil on canvas mounted over board, Cape Ann Museum, Gloucester, 2019.2, gift of Jean Stanley Dise, 1964, accessed November 2026, https://capeannmuseum.quartexcollections.com/resources/artists-in-the-collection-of-the-cape-ann-museuma-partial-list/mary-blood-mellen-18191886. Data-verified from the Cape Ann Museum’s artist page, which gives Mellen’s dates as 1819–1886 and records that her brother-in-law, the Universalist minister William R. G. Mellen, took the Gloucester pulpit in 1855, the family connection thought to have brought her into Lane’s circle.
